# Sentriq

Sentriq is an AI-powered platform designed to automate the collection, processing, analysis, and structuring of information from multiple sources.

The primary goal of the project is to identify truly relevant information within large volumes of data, eliminate information noise, and deliver timely, structured, and actionable insights.

The platform initially focuses on Cybersecurity, Threat Intelligence, AI Security, and IT Infrastructure, with the potential to expand into other domains.

Concept

Sentriq aggregates information from various sources, including Telegram, RSS feeds, specialized media, GitHub, and external APIs.

At its core is an LLM-powered AI analysis engine designed to go beyond simple news summarization by evaluating the significance of events, identifying relationships, analyzing context, and explaining why specific developments matter.

The system aims to generate personalized intelligence digests, delivering the most relevant information in a clear and accessible format.


# Project Goal

To build a versatile intelligence platform that reduces the time spent manually monitoring information sources and transforms fragmented data into Actionable Intelligence — meaningful insights that support situational awareness and informed decision-making.

Status: Concept / Early Development
